# **Report Structure & Content Requirements**

## Abstract:

- Summarize the prevalence of PFAS, primary health concerns, and the consensus of the review.

## Introduction & Chemical Background:

- Briefly define PFAS chemistry (the carbon-fluorine bond strength).
- Distinguish between long-chain and short-chain compounds.

## Exposure Pathways & Toxicokinetics:

- Analyze primary routes of exposure (drinking water, food packaging, dust, dermal).
- Discuss bioaccumulation, biomagnification, and serum half-lives in humans vs. animals.

## System-Specific Health Impacts (The Core Analysis):

- Dedicate a subsection to each of the following systems. For each, distinguish between "sufficient evidence," "limited/suggestive evidence," and "inadequate evidence" based on major regulatory assessments (EPA, IARC, EFSA).
  - Immunotoxicity: Effects on vaccine response and antibody production (specifically in children).
  - Carcinogenicity: Links to kidney and testicular cancer (referencing the IARC classification).
  - Developmental & Reproductive Toxicity: Low birth weight, pre-eclampsia, and fertility outcomes.
  - Metabolic & Endocrine Disruption: Lipid metabolism (cholesterol levels), thyroid disease, and liver enzyme alterations.

## Mechanisms of Action:

- Discuss the biological plausibility of these effects (e.g., PPAR-$\alpha$ activation, oxidative stress, epigenetics).

## The "GenX" Problem:

- Evaluate available data on newer replacement chemicals. Are they safer, or do they exhibit regrettable substitution characteristics?

## Regulatory Context & Knowledge Gaps:

- Briefly mention current Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) proposed by the US EPA or EU REACH restrictions.
- Identify critical gaps in current research (e.g., mixture toxicity).

## Conclusion:

- Provide a high-level summary of the public health implications.
