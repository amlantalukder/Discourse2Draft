# **Required Report Structure**

## **1. Executive Summary**

- Summarize chemical features of **ortho-phthalates** and major industrial uses.
- Highlight key health concerns, especially as **endocrine-disrupting chemicals (EDCs)**.

## **2. Classification and Exposure Assessment**

- Distinguish:
  - **Low–Molecular-Weight (LMW)** phthalates (**DMP, DEP**)
  - **High–Molecular-Weight (HMW)** phthalates (**DEHP, DINP**)
- Describe sources: **PVC plasticizers**, personal-care products, medical devices.
- Describe exposure pathways: **ingestion, inhalation, dermal, parenteral**.

## **3. Toxicokinetics (ADME)**

- **Absorption:** bioavailability across routes.
- **Metabolism:** hydrolysis → monoesters (e.g., **DEHP → MEHP**) → oxidative metabolites → **glucuronidation**.
  - Emphasize **monoesters** as the primary **biologically active toxicants**.
- **Excretion:** urinary metabolites, biomonitoring value, half-lives.

## **4. Molecular Mechanisms of Action**

- **Endocrine disruption:** anti-androgenic effects; **Phthalate Syndrome**.
- **Receptor interaction:** PPARα/γ activation; possible **AhR** involvement.
- **Oxidative stress:** mitochondrial dysfunction, ROS.

## **5. Organ-Specific Toxicity (Evidence Review)**

- **Reproductive/Developmental:** reduced **AGD**, hypospadias, Leydig cell impairment, fertility outcomes.
- **Neurodevelopmental:** associations with **ADHD**, ASD-like traits, cognitive outcomes.
- **Metabolic/Hepatic:** obesity risk, insulin resistance, hepatic peroxisome proliferation.

## **6. Vulnerable Populations**

- Focus on **first 1000 days**.
- Discuss **placental** and **lactational** transfer.

## **7. Regulatory Landscape & Tolerable Limits**

- Compare **RfD (EPA)** vs. **TDI (EFSA, ECHA)**.
- Discuss cumulative risks, mixture toxicity, **dose-addition** models.

## **8. Emerging Science & Alternatives**

- Assess substitutes (**DEHT/DOTP**, **DINCH**).
- Consider whether they are **regrettable substitutions** or safer alternatives.

## **9. Conclusion & Future Directions**

- Identify knowledge gaps: **transgenerational epigenetics**, mixture effects, long-term epidemiology.
